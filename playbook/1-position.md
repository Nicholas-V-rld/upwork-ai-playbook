# 1. Position the profile

## The model this copies
A US WordPress freelancer went from 0 to 46 jobs, 100% Job Success and Rising Talent in about 8 weeks (public profile, read October 2026). His jobs were almost all small and fixed-price: $15, $170, $200, $300, $450, $700, $1,400. Why it worked:

1. **The headline sells a problem, not a tool.** "Slow, hacked or broken WordPress site? I find the real cause, fix it without breaking what works, and show you the numbers before and after."
2. **Every claim has a number.** "Mobile PageSpeed 27 to 84." "10,614 spam posts removed." No adjectives doing the work.
3. **One narrow niche, many ways in.** Speed, malware, broken site, migration, technical SEO, WooCommerce, page builders, monthly care. All of them are fix jobs, and all are fast to deliver.
4. **He takes the small job.** A $15 fix still earns a 5★ review and adds to the job count. Early on, the number of reviews matters more than contract size.
5. **He upsells into retainers.** Small fixes turn into "monthly care for 3 sites."
6. **The process builds trust.** "Backup first. Baseline measured. Every change logged and reversible. Plain-English report at the end."

## Profile template (fill it with your own facts)

**Title (≤70 characters):** `<Platform> & <Tool> Developer | Fix, Speed Up & Build | <Adjacent tool>`

**Overview:**
```
<Problem question>? I find the real cause and fix it without breaking what works, or build <thing> in <tool>. You see the numbers before and after.

I'm <name>, <one honest line about your background and what makes harder problems normal for you>.

WHAT I FIX AND BUILD
✅ <Problem area>: <specific symptoms clients search for>
… 6–8 lines …

HOW I WORK
1. Full backup first, and I measure the starting point.
2. Changes on staging when there is one. Every change logged and reversible.
3. Tested on phone and desktop.
4. A plain-English report: what was wrong, what changed, the numbers before and after.

Small jobs are welcome. <One bug / one page> is a fine place to start. Fixed price agreed up front, paid through Upwork escrow. <Location + honest time-zone overlap.>

RECENT RESULTS   ← add after your first 3 jobs. Real numbers only.

Send me <the link> and tell me <what's broken>. I'll reply with where I'd start and a fixed price.
```

**Skills:** fill all 20 slots with niche skills clients filter by. Upwork's skill resolver drops any name that isn't in its list.

**Rate:** stay low until you have about 10 five-star reviews, then raise it.

## Rules
- Real numbers only. If you don't have before/after results yet, leave that section out until you do.
- One person per account. Every fact has to be true of whoever is doing the work.
- If you're changing niche, keep the old one alive in an Upwork specialized profile.
